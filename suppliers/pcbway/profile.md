# PCBWay

## Overview
PCB fabrication, 3D printing, CNC, sheet metal, injection molding.

## API
- Partner API: https://www.pcbway.com/api_cooperation.html
- PCB quoting: Available
- Assembly API: Under construction
- 3D printing: Available

## Capabilities
- PCB fabrication
- PCBA (limited API)
- 3D printing (FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, SLM)
- CNC machining
- Sheet metal
- Injection molding
- Rapid prototyping ($25 minimum)

## Pricing
- 3D print: from $4.98
- CNC: from $25
- PCB: competitive with JLCPCB

## MOQ
- 3D print: 1 piece
- CNC: 1 piece
- PCB: 5 pieces typical

## Strengths
- Broader mechanical capabilities than JLCPCB
- CNC and injection molding
- Good for enclosure/mechanical parts
- Rapid prototyping service

## Weaknesses
- Assembly API not ready
- No component sourcing (unlike JLCPCB)
- Higher minimum order for some services
